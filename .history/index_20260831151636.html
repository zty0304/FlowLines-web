Overview

We introduce a structure-aware rendering framework for stylized hair synthesis, explicitly disentangling geometry and color while preserving natural motion and coherence across views.

  • Flow-guided structure modeling
  • Color-preserving detail synthesis
  • Improved controllability and realism

Abstract

We present a method for generating coherent anime hair with disentangled structure and color attributes. The core idea is to separately model flow-based geometric structure and appearance-based color information so that each component can be controlled and optimized independently. This allows our approach to preserve detailed strand motion while avoiding color leakage and visual artifacts. Extensive experiments demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves strong performance on quality, controllability, and stability compared with recent baselines.

Method

01

Structure Modeling

We estimate flow-aware hair structures that capture continuity, direction, and local variation across the strand field.

02

Color Disentanglement

Appearance cues are decoupled from geometric flow to preserve hue consistency while retaining expressive highlight transitions.

03

Consistent Rendering

A joint optimization stage enforces spatio-temporal coherence and reduces identity drift during reconstruction and synthesis.
